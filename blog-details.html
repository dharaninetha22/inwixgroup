AWS, Azure or GCP: How to Choose the Right Cloud Migration Path

Cloud is at the core of what Inwix Group does - we plan, build and run cloud environments that make businesses faster, more resilient and easier to scale. Migrating for the first time, tightening an existing setup, or extending what you already have, our team delivers expert service across AWS, Google Cloud Platform (GCP) and Microsoft Azure.

Choosing the right cloud platform isn't about picking the "best" provider — it's about matching the platform's strengths to your business needs.

- Inwix Techventures

Amazon Web Services (AWS) — put Amazon Web Services to work with cloud solutions shaped around your workloads, built to run faster, cost less and scale without friction. Our AWS practice covers migration and deployment, fully managed services with round-the-clock monitoring, serverless architectures, big data and AI solutions, plus security and compliance.

Google Cloud Platform (GCP) — tap into Google's advanced cloud infrastructure for sharper business operations, covering cloud strategy and consulting, application modernisation on Kubernetes/GKE, data analytics and machine learning, and multi-cloud or hybrid architectures.

Microsoft Azure — extend what your business can do with the flexibility and horsepower of Microsoft Azure, covering migration and optimisation, hybrid cloud architectures, AI and IoT integration, DevOps automation, and enterprise-grade security through Azure Sentinel and Defender.

Whatever platform is right for your business, Inwix's certified engineers guide the planning, the migration and the ongoing management of your cloud environment - with security and governance designed in from the outset.
